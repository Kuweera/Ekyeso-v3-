📖 USER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY EKYESO

🎯 THE AIM

Ekyeso is a two-player Busoga board game. Capture the opponent's playable tokens until the opponent has no playable tokens left.

🟤 MOVING A TOKEN

  • A normal token moves one connected step to an empty intersection.
  • Movement follows the lines drawn on the board.
  • After a normal token reaches the opponent's first/final line and settles there at the end of its turn, it becomes a King.

🍽️ EATING / CAPTURING

  • A normal token eats by jumping over an adjacent opponent token along a connected line and landing in an empty position beyond it.
  • If a capture is mandatory, the player must make a capture.
  • After the first capture, another available capture may be continued, but the player can choose to finish the turn.
  • An eaten token leaves the board and is placed on the captor's side.

👑 KINGS

  • A King can move along the real board lines in any allowed direction.
  • A King may resist eating a normal token, but it cannot resist a legal capture of another King.
  • Choose the King long-distance rule in Settings: After 1 King capture OR 3 King moves, or Immediately on crowning.
  • Until a King has earned its energy, it captures like a normal token: only an opponent right next to it. Long-distance captures and moves unlock after its first capture as a King, or after 3 King moves.
  • Long-distance movement still follows the actual board lines; it is not teleportation.
  • Draw: if each player is left with exactly one King and nobody captures within 10 moves each, the game ends in a draw.

🤖 COMPUTER MODE

In Human vs Computer, PLAYER A = HUMAN and PLAYER B = COMPUTER. The board and turn indicator show the role clearly.

⚙️ MOVEMENT SPEED

Token movement and capture animation speed can be changed at any time in Settings.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

If a player refuses a mandatory capture, the applicable token is forfeited according to the game rules.
